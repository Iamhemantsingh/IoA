## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a digital standard for a premier archaeological research institution and museum repository. It bridges sovereign archival authority with world-class academic rigour, projecting an institutional gravity on par with the Bodleian Library, the British Museum, and the Archaeological Survey of India’s apex research facilities.

### Brand Identity & Mood
- **Archetype:** The Custodian Scholar — ancient wisdom preserved through scientific precision, field excavation rigour, and architectural majesty.
- **Atmosphere:** Monumental, grounded, enduring, and contemplative. Textures evoking hand-hewn Dholpur and Red Agra sandstone are paired with refined scholarly typography and bronze detailing.
- **Audience:** Global archaeologists, research scholars, postgraduate students, cultural preservationists, and civil service curators seeking academic diplomas, field excavation reports, and archival collections.

### Design Movement: Classical Monumentalism with Academic Modernity
The visual direction fuses tactile architectural heritage with modern editorial clarity:
- Sharp, deliberate framing inspired by epigraphic stone stelae and lintel proportions.
- Subdued parchment and sandstone surfaces rather than sterile digital whites.
- High-contrast academic legibility supporting multi-script typographic hierarchy (Latin and Devanagari).
- Structural rhythm rooted in monumental Indian architecture: stepped archways, symmetrical balance, and restrained bronze accents.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from historic quarry materials, ancient pottery slip, weathered ASI bronze crests, and archival palm-leaf manuscripts.

### Palette Roles & Balance
- **Red Sandstone / Terracotta (`#8C3B24`):** The primary institutional anchor. Used for primary navigation states, active tab markers, hero banners, crest elements, and high-priority academic CTAs.
- **Deep Heritage Charcoal / Bronze (`#2A2421`):** The dominant neutral-dark tone. Replaces standard interface blacks for body text, footers, academic seals, and structural hairline borders.
- **Warm Muted Ochre / Antique Gold (`#C68B45`):** The tertiary flourish. Used for scholarly distinctions, gold filigree dividers, accreditation badges, and curated research highlights.
- **Warm Dholpur Sandstone / Vellum (`#F7F3EE`):** The default canvas base. Provides an organic parchment warmth that relieves screen fatigue during deep reading and catalog study.
- **Dholpur Medium Sandstone (`#EAD8C3`):** Surface tone for sub-cards, metadata chips, table headers, and structural panel borders.
- **Field Sage Accent (`#3B5342`):** Botanical and ecological survey indicator used for field excavations, active environmental monitoring tags, and verified laboratory certifications.

### Contrast & Functional Tones
- **Canvas Base:** `#FBF9F5` (Parchment White)
- **Surface Elevation 1:** `#F7F3EE` (Light Dholpur Sandstone)
- **Surface Elevation 2:** `#EFE7DC` (Muted Sandstone Buff)
- **Text Primary:** `#2A2421` (Contrast ratio > 11.5:1 against `#F7F3EE`)
- **Text Secondary (Scribe):** `#5E534D` (Contrast ratio > 5.5:1)
- **Subtle Hairline Border:** `#D8CABA`

## Typography

The typographic system pairs an authoritative, stone-inscriptional serif with a razor-sharp modern grotesque sans-serif.

### Type Hierarchy & Pairing
- **Headlines & Section Titles (`Noto Serif`):** Conveys the weight of ASI historical scholarship, stone edicts, and archival publications. Set with balanced tracking and controlled line heights to reflect classical typesetting.
- **Academic Coursework, Body & Data (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures high scan-speed, clear rendering across dense course curricula, excavation logs, and administrative timetables.
- **Bilingual Typographic Harmony:** When displaying Hindi / Devanagari text alongside English, use Noto Serif Devanagari for headings to preserve stroke modulation and Noto Sans Devanagari alongside Plus Jakarta Sans for body text to maintain optical height parity.

### Editorial Treatments
- Section headers can be paired with an uppercase overline in `label-sm` with `0.08em` letter-spacing, rendered in Tertiary Ochre (`#C68B45`).
- Academic course titles should use `title-lg` or `headline-sm` with strict truncation at 2 lines to preserve grid uniformity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model uses a 12-column symmetrical grid, echoing the balance and axial symmetry of classical monument architecture.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥ 1200px):** 12 columns, 32px gutters, max-width 1280px, dynamic outer margins centered.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 24px gutters, 32px page margins.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, 16px page margins.

### Rhythms & Vertical Pacing
- Sections alternate deliberately between open Dholpur sandstone (`#F7F3EE`) and warm parchment canvas (`#FBF9F5`) to establish spatial chapters without hard dividers.
- Section headers follow a mandatory `space-2xl` top padding and `space-lg` bottom margin to foster Oxford/Ivy-league editorial gravitas.
- Tight data modules (such as monthly academic calendars and syllabus tables) use compact `space-xs` and `space-sm` increments inside structural bordered containers.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than synthetic multi-layered drop shadows, this design system expresses depth through tactile stone layering, subtle architectural borders, and warm ambient light reminiscent of open-air courtyards.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Surface Level 0 (Ground/Canvas):** Natural parchment tone (`#FBF9F5`).
- **Surface Level 1 (Panels & Structural Blocks):** Dholpur Sandstone (`#F7F3EE`) bounded by crisp 1px hairline rules (`#D8CABA`). No drop shadow.
- **Surface Level 2 (Interactive Cards & Course Tiles):** White/Cream surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a warm, low-opacity ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.06), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Surface Level 3 (Floating Modals, Search Overlays, Flyout Drawers):** High-density cream base with a deep terracotta-bronze ambient haze: `box-shadow: 0 16px 36px -4px rgba(42, 36, 33, 0.16), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(140, 59, 36, 0.08)`.
- **Architectural Framing (The Lintel Border):** Prominent cards and academic wing widgets feature an optional top border rule of 3px in Solid Red Sandstone (`#8C3B24`) or Antique Gold (`#C68B45`), establishing formal institutional grounding.

## Shapes

The shape geometry is crisp, disciplined, and rooted in dressed stone ashlar masonry. Curves are minimal and restrained; large playful radii are avoided to preserve formal academic authority.

### Radii Hierarchy
- **Base Elements (Buttons, Inputs, Badges, Tabs):** 4px (`0.25rem`) — provides microscopic softening while retaining rectangular monumentality.
- **Containers & Course Cards:** 6px to 8px maximum (`0.375rem` – `0.5rem`) — maintains structural alignment in multi-column grids.
- **Stone Medallions & Diamond Motifs:** Academic iconography (e.g., Museology, Epigraphy, Conservation) uses rotated 45-degree diamond cartouches (`transform: rotate(45deg)`) with centered glyphs, referencing classic ASI monument survey markers.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Institutional Button:** Solid Red Agra Sandstone background (`#8C3B24`), text in pure white (`#FFFFFF`), font `label-lg`, height 44px, padding 0 24px, 4px border radius. Hover state darkens to `#722E1B` with subtle translateY(-1px).
- **Secondary Academic Button:** Transparent background, 1.5px solid Heritage Charcoal border (`#2A2421`), text in `#2A2421`. Hover fills with `#2A2421` and text transitions to `#F7F3EE`.
- **Tertiary Link / Text Action:** Antique Gold (`#C68B45`) or Red Sandstone (`#8C3B24`) with an underline offset of 4px and a trailing arrow glyph (`→`).

### 2. Course & Research Cards
- **Construction:** Structured vertical or horizontal card wrapped in a 1px border (`#D8CABA`) on background `#FFFFFF`.
- **Header Asset:** Aspect ratio 16:9 archival photography with a subtle warm sepia/sunlight grading filter.
- **Card Content:** Top department chip (`label-sm` in `#8C3B24` or `#3B5342`), course title in `title-md`, followed by structured academic metadata: duration, credit units, and field excavation requirements accompanied by monochromatic heritage bronze icons.

### 3. Academic Calendar & Activity Feed
- **Header:** Dark Heritage Bronze panel (`#2A2421`) with month-year pagination and gold lettering (`#C68B45`).
- **Calendar Matrix:** Clean grid on `#F7F3EE`, active selected date in filled Red Sandstone circle (`#8C3B24`).
- **Activity Event Pill:** Two-part horizontal split item: left block in dark bronze (`#2A2421`) showing day/month in bold cream text; right block in `#FFFFFF` showing the event title, category, and time.

### 4. Department & Wing Badges (Diamond Cartouches)
- Distinctive ASI service indicators (e.g., Structural Conservation, Epigraphy, Museology).
- Rendered as a 64x64px diamond border (rotated square, 1.5px stroke in `#C68B45`), enclosing clean architectural line icons, paired with `label-md` descriptive labels below.

### 5. Form Fields & Search Inputs
- **Base Input:** Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#D8CABA`, 40px height, 12px horizontal padding, font `body-md`. Focus state transitions border to `#8C3B24` with a 2px outer ring in `rgba(140, 59, 36, 0.15)`.
- **Academic Filters & Sorting Chips:** Background `#EAD8C3`, text `#2A2421`, borderless, 4px radius. Active state switches to `#8C3B24` with white text.

### 6. Institutional Header & Official Bar
- **Apex Sovereign Strip:** Government of India / Ministry of Culture top bar in deep charcoal (`#1D1917`) with official bilingual seals, accessibility text sizing controls, and English/Hindi language switchers.
- **Main Institute Masthead:** Warm Dholpur Sandstone base (`#F7F3EE`), centered or left-aligned bilingual institute crest, primary serif navigation links with 2px bottom indicator on active states.
