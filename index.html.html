About the Institute & Campus Constituents

schoolAbout Institute

This building of the institute was inaugurated on 9th March 2019 by Hon’ble Prime Minister Sh. Narendra Modi with the mandate that this Institute shall provide archaeological opportunities to all and play a major role in furthering the understanding of the archaeological and historical past of the Indian subcontinent. With the aim to remain in the forefront of international research in archaeological sciences, besides promoting best practices in the management of cultural heritage and in the care and preservation of archaeological artifacts and to interact with State Departments, Museums, Universities and other Archaeological/ Cultural Institutions in furthering the cause of social, political and economic context of archaeology. An additional objective is to promote interaction in Archaeology and Applied Sciences and provide leadership in curriculum planning and to develop training programmes for both in-service professionals & pre-service connoisseurs.
